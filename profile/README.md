# Quentatur

Notes on J.R.R. Tolkien’s legendarium, and the code behind them.

> For he that attempteth this shall prove but mine instrument in the devising of things more wonderful, which he himself hath not imagined.
>
> *The Silmarillion*, [`S AL.009`](https://cite.digitaltolkien.com/S/AL.009/)

The notes live at **[quentatur.com](https://quentatur.com)**. They cite the
Professor’s works with the [Digital Tolkien Project](https://digitaltolkien.com/)’s
edition-independent references, and this organization aims to complement
and contribute to [the project’s work](https://github.com/digitaltolkien).

Work under the Quentatur name, here and in other projects, comes from
[@aleberriz](https://github.com/aleberriz).

<sub>An unofficial fan project, not affiliated with the Tolkien Estate or any rights holder.</sub>
